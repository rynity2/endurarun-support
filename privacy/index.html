EnduraRun for iPhone and Apple Watch

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

EnduraRun is a running metronome and coach. It's built so that your information stays yours.

What we collect

Nothing. EnduraRun has no accounts, no servers, no analytics and no advertising or tracking. The developer never receives your data.

What stays on your devices

  • Run history, goals and profile (for example cadence, duration, heart rate during runs, and the answers you give during setup) are stored on your iPhone. If you're signed in to iCloud, they sync through your private iCloud account, which only you can access.
  • Apple Health: only if you turn on "Connect Apple Health". EnduraRun then saves your runs as workouts, and reads: heart rate and resting heart rate (to show it live, power Dynamic Tempo and set your zones), workouts recorded by other apps or your Apple Watch (so your plan reflects all your training), body weight (to estimate calories) and cardio fitness. What it reads stays on your iPhone. Health data is never used for advertising and never leaves your devices except through Apple's own Health and iCloud services.
  • Photos and videos: the ones you choose to attach to a run are copied into the app's own folder on your iPhone. EnduraRun uses Apple's photo picker, so it never has access to the rest of your library, and it doesn't upload them anywhere. Saving a share card to Photos adds one image and reads nothing.
  • Apple Watch: the iPhone and Watch apps talk directly to each other (cadence, start/stop, heart rate, and runs the Watch recorded on its own). Nothing passes through a server.
  • Location: only if you turn on GPS pace, to measure pace and distance during outdoor runs. It isn't stored as a route and isn't shared.
  • Motion: step cadence during runs, processed on your iPhone.
  • Bluetooth: to connect to a heart-rate strap you choose.
  • On-device wording: on iPhones with Apple Intelligence, coaching text may be reworded by Apple's on-device model. This happens entirely on your iPhone.

Deleting your data

Delete individual runs in the History tab (their photos go with them), or delete the app to remove all local data. To remove the iCloud copy: iPhone Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage → EnduraRun. Health data can be managed in the Health app.

Children

EnduraRun isn't directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Medical disclaimer

EnduraRun provides general fitness guidance. It isn't medical advice and doesn't diagnose any condition.

Contact

Questions: send a request from the EnduraRun support page. Support requests are posted on GitHub and are public. What you write there is handled under GitHub's own privacy policy, not by the app.
